# Downloaded GLB model sources

All five models were downloaded from Innerscene's public library, which marks them CC0. They are generic visualization models, not ScadaWatt manufacturer CAD.

- pickup.glb — Crew-cab pickup with open bed: https://www.innerscene.com/tools/library/3d-parts/crew-cab-pickup-with-open-bed-4f02e0cc
- worker.glb — Drew, hard hat and hi-vis: https://www.innerscene.com/tools/library/3d-parts/drew-3d-person-construction-worker-with-hard-hat-and-hi-vis-a4aa98af
- walker.glb — Sam, Walk_Brisk skeleton animation: https://www.innerscene.com/tools/library/3d-parts/brisk-walk-sam-3d-person-animated-bed03fa4
- operator.glb — Seated office figure: https://www.innerscene.com/tools/library/3d-parts/seated-office-figure-79182d38
- sofa.glb — Lowline three-seat sofa: https://www.innerscene.com/tools/library/3d-parts/lowline-low-three-seat-sofa-4bb27e8c

People: Innerscene, adapted from MakeHuman/MPFB CC0 body/skin/clothing sources, as documented on source pages and embedded GLB extras. Preserve these embedded credits. All geometry, skinning, clips, and original PBR materials were retained. Embedded textures were capped at 1024px, PNG retained for alpha/data. Models are uniformly fitted for the scene. The animated person receives a head-mounted copy of the construction model's hardhat at runtime. The walking clip is authored motion, not motion capture. Static models remain static.

Source previews were inspected separately; the integrated site has not been visually verified on a GPU in this environment. No third-party screenshot is included in the deliverable.
